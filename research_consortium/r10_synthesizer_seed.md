### Working Concept: "AETHER"

**Acronym:** Ambient Environmental Three-dimensional Holographic Emitter

**Core Innovation:** Invisible airborne metamaterial particles that selectively retroreflect and modulate light upon laser activation

**How it works:**
1. **Setup:** Ceiling/wall emitters project structured light
2. **Particles:** Micro-corner cubes suspended in room air currents
3. **Activation:** Laser pulse train selectively illuminates particles
4. **Perception:** Eye integrates scattered light as 3D image

**Key Technical Components:**
- Acoustic levitation field (inaudible, gentle)
- Photonic metamaterial particles (glass corner cubes with electrochromic coating)
- Femtosecond pulse train for voxel addressing
- Distributed emitter array for multi-view

### Revolutionary Elements

1. **No glasses:** Free-space viewing
2. **No screen:** Particles are the screen
3. **Room-scale:** Entire volume is display
4. **Energy efficient:** Passive particles, only illuminate what's needed
