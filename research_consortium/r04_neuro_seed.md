# R04: Neuroscientist - Cycle 0

## Perceptual Requirements & Neural Pathways

### What the Visual System Actually Needs

**Temporal Requirements:**
- Persistence of vision: ~10-20ms
- Critical flicker fusion: 60-90Hz
- Saccadic masking: 40ms window

**Spatial Requirements:**
- Visual acuity: 1 arcmin = ~0.3mM at 1m
- 1080p at 1m requires 2M pixels
- Need depth cues: occlusion, motion parallax

### Bi-Direct Stimulation Approaches

**1. Phosphene Induction**
- Mechanism: Magnetic stimulation of visual cortex (TMS-like)
- Requirements: ~1-2 Tesla pulses
- Can create "stars" in visual field without eyes
- Safety: Unknown long-term effects

**2. Retinal Direct Stimulation**
- Mechanism: Current injection via scleral electrodes
- Requirements: microamp currents
- Resolution: Limited (~32x32 demonstrated)

**3. Optogenetics (Speculative)**
- Mechanism: Viral delivery of light-sensitive opsins
- Requirements: Near-infrared light penetration
- Timeline: Decades

### Perceptual Hack
Rather than creating physical light, could we exploit:
- Afterimages (retinal burn-in)
- Entoptic phenomena (self-generated light)
- Neural entrainment

### Safety Paramount
Any neural approach requires extensive validation
