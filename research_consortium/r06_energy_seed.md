# R06: Energy Specialist - Cycle 0

## Energy Analysis

### Laser Filamentation Power Budget

**Per Voxel Requirements:**
- Filament generation: ~10mJ pulses
- Repetition rate: 60Hz per voxel
- Power: 0.6W per voxel (average)
- 1M voxels: 600kW PULSE power
- But femtosecond: duty cycle lowers average

**CPA Safety Mechanism:**
- Stretch pulse: ps duration → lower peak intensity
- Compress at output: safer transport
- Average power manageable

### Wireless Power Considerations

**For Smart Dust:**
- RFID-like power harvesting: µW per particle
- Requires high-field region
- Safety: FCC limits on RF exposure

**For Levitation:**
- Acoustic: ~10W/m³ for mid-air levitation
- Magnetic: Superconducting traps
- Optical: Optical tweezers (single particles)

### Power Delivery Options

1. **Wired Grid**
   - Ceiling-mounted power rails
   - Dust particles have micro-capacitors
   - Charge via induction when near grid

2. **Laser Power Beaming**
   - Infrared laser to photovoltaic receivers
   - On smart dust particles
   - Requires precise tracking

3. **Ambience Harvesting**
   - Solar: ~100W/m² indoors
   - Thermal gradients: minimal
   - Radio frequency: minimal
