# R02: Materials Scientist - Cycle 0

## Materials for Invisible Display Media

### Candidate: Photonic Smart Dust

**Concept:** Micro-scale corner cube retroreflectors with embedded modulators

**Specifications Needed:**
- Size: 10-100 microns (invisible at distance)
- Material: Glass/polymer with metamaterial coating
- Modulation: Electrochromic or MEMS-based
- Suspension: Acoustic levitation or microdrone swarm

**Power Requirements:**
- Modulation: ~nW per particle
- Levitation: Acoustic ~10W/m³
- Total density: 10^6 particles/m³ for 1080p equivalent

### Alternative: Phase-Change Aerosol

**Concept:** Droplets that scatter only when activated
- Base state: Transparent
- Activated via UV/heat/chemical trigger
- Reversible phase change

**Materials:**
- Liquid crystals (cholesteric)
- Thermochromic polymers
- Gas-to-plasma (argon dots)

### Critical Challenge
How to maintain stable cloud without being visible/breathable?
