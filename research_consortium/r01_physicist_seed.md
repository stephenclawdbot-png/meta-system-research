# R01: Theoretical Physicist - Cycle 0

## Physics of Atmospheric Light Localization

### Approaches to "Floating" Light

1. **Laser Filamentation**
   - Femtosecond pulses self-focus via Kerr effect
   - Ionizes air creating plasma "white filament"
   - Intensities: 10^14 W/cm², requires CPA
   - Each filament = 1 voxel, white emission

2. **Acousto-Optic Interaction**
   - Ultrasound creates temporary density gradients
   - Laser refracts at acoustic pressure nodes
   - Requires GHz acoustic frequencies

3. **Nonlinear Optical Interactions**
   - Four-wave mixing for phase conjugation
   - Could create "anchor points" in space

### Critical Constraints
- Laser safety: Must stay Class 1 (eye-safe)
- Filamentation requires very high intensity but short duration
- Atmospheric turbulence limits coherence

### Blockers Identified
1. Energy requirement per voxel is massive
2. Refresh rate limited by plasma recombination (~ns)
3. Color control in filaments is limited (white/plasma)

### Potential Breakthrough
Chirped pulse femtosecond arrays: Multiple filaments from single beam via beam shaping? Time-multiplexed voxels?
