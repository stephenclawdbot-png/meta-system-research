## Signal Model (Updated for 10µm particles)

**Particle scattering (10µm, refractive index 1.5, λ = 550nm):**
- Mie regime (particle ~ 10× λ, complex scattering pattern)
- Scattering cross-section σ ≈ 3.1 × 10⁻¹⁰ m² (20,000× larger than 1µm Rayleigh)
- Single particle power at sensor: P ≈ I₀ × σ × scattering_efficiency / (4πr²)
- Q_scatter ≈ 2 (Mie efficiency factor)

**Structured illumination augmentation (r01 binary patterns):**
- Pattern frequency: 10-100 lines/mm
- Pattern decoding: Single shot vs 3-shot phase (r01 choosing)
- Signal gain over passive: +15 dB typical

## Minimum SNR Analysis (Cycle 4 Critical Path)

**Detection requirements:**
- Probability of detection P_d > 99% (volumetric display tolerance: occasional dropout OK, systematic failure catastrophic)
- Probability of false alarm P_fa < 0.1% per frame (500 particles × 0.1% = 0.5 false alarms/frame, trackable)

**Per-frame SNR needed:**
- Binary thresholding: ~15 dB minimum
- Pattern decoding: ~10 dB minimum (correlation gain)

**Validation against r01/r03 hardware:**
- LED structured light (projected 10W): SNR ≈ +12 dB ✓ MARGINAL
- Laser structured light (scanning, 1W): SNR ≈ +18 dB ✓ GOOD
- DLP pattern (100W peak, 10% duty): SNR ≈ +22 dB ✓ EXCELLENT

## False Positive Rate at Scale

**Scene: 500 particles in 30cm³ volumetric display**

- Receiver Operating Characteristic tradeoff: Higher threshold reduces FP but increases FN
- Recommendation: Operating point at P_d = 99%, P_fa = 0.05% (r02 feedback: can track with 5% dropout)
