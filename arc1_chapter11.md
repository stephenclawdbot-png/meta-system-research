# ASHES OF THE DAWN
## Chapter 11: The Bombardment Begins

Arin returned to the war-camp with dire news: Crassus planned a bombardment at dawn. The Keth scrambled to prepare defensive measures while Arin grappled with the realization that Crassus could partially sense the Hollow.

**Key Events:**
- Arin reports imperial plans to Torvin
- Darro confirms Crassus's Threadmeld abilities may detect Hollow echoes
- Keth prepare underground shelters and evacuation routes
- Lira trains Arin in basic combat while maintaining Hollow state
- First imperial artillery rounds hit at false dawn
